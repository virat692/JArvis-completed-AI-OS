# JARVIS AI OS — Design System & UI/UX Guidelines
**Source of Truth:** `PRD.md`  
**Document:** `design.md`

---

## 1. Visual Identity & Atmosphere

- **Theme Spectrum:** Cyber Blue (Default Holographic), Slate Carbon, Emerald Minimal, Gold Luxury.
- **Background Contrast:** Deep obsidian blacks (`#020712`, `#000000`) paired with radiant cyan glows (`#00f2ff`), amber highlights, and emerald signals.
- **Typography Pairing:**
  - Display / Headings: `Orbitron` (High-tech geometric monospace display)
  - Body Text / Reading: `Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Crisp, high-legibility sans-serif)

---

## 2. Layout & Surface Principles

1. **Anti-Slop Directives:**
   - No purple-to-blue generic SaaS gradients.
   - No nested cards or heavy 1px boxed side-tabs.
   - Full viewport edge-to-edge canvas with borderless seamless transitions for all modal surfaces.
2. **Dynamic Presentation Surface:**
   - 5 specialized content dimensions: Code, Data, Structure, Paragraph, Overview.
   - Fluid typography with calculated reading time metrics and syntax highlighting.
3. **Audio-Tactile Feedback:**
   - Custom Web Audio API synthesizer generating real-time acoustic feedback for UI interactions.
